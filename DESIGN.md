# Research Atlas design contract

References inspected: the default-branch HTML, CSS and research page from https://github.com/postech-psi/postech-psi.github.io and the index, guide and stylesheet from https://github.com/postech-psi/test-results. These are design references; this is not a PSI or POSTECH official publication.

Carry forward the large Pretendard typography, open whitespace, deliberate two-column reading layouts, quiet rules, accessible controls, substantive diagrams, and explicit evidence/validation labels. Replace the monochrome/magenta accents with a pastel-blue system. Do not reuse PSI logos, rocket photography, or affiliation marks.

Palette: paper #FFFFFF, blue wash #F0F6FC, pastel blue #C7E0F6, selected blue #94BFE4, ink #10283F, action #245F91. All small text uses ink or dark blue, never pastel text on white. Supplementary method families use muted lavender and teal only in map legends.

Type: locally bundled SIL-licensed Pretendard, 16px body/1.65, 14px secondary, 38–70px headings. Long reading text stays around 68ch. Sans-serif throughout to match the references. No all-caps eyebrow decoration or arbitrary numbered sections.

Layout:
Header: Research Atlas / Field map / Encyclopedia / Scholars / Papers / Learn
Overview: large research question + concise introduction
Map: interactive 18-topic diagram + selected-topic explanation
Below: selected reading routes + evidence/coverage summary
Chapter: local contents rail / long-form explanation / connected papers and scholars
Directory: persistent search/filter controls / compact rows / full scholar pages

The memorable element is a usable field map. No giant generic KPI hero, floating decorative shapes, gradient washes or identical marketing cards. Research counts belong in the coverage area. The reference sites' diagrams become a conceptual network rather than aerospace decoration.

Connection semantics: concept edges are editorial explanations. Person edges distinguish sourced PhD, postdoc, named lab and coauthored-paper relationships; they never imply personal access. Shared-paper links are undirected and keep individual identity evidence. Historical/context-only people do not become eligible application targets. Topic matching is a navigational association, not evidence of collaboration.

Review of design against brief: retain the references' visual restraint while replacing their launch-video opening with the actual object the user needs to explore: a map of research. Dense record tables are retained only where comparison benefits from them; chapters use open prose and specific examples.
